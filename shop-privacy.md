# Shop Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 23 September 2026_

This policy covers the IDNTEQ Shop web page at {{ site.url }}/shop/ and the IDNTEQ Shop Android app, which shows the same page.

## What we collect

Nothing leaves your device. The Shop stores your cart, orders, reviews, promo codes and newsletter sign-ups only in your browser's local storage on your device. This includes the name, email and shipping address you enter at checkout. IDNTEQ has no server that receives this information and can't see it.

## Payments

The Shop is a demo. It doesn't take payments or ship products, and it never asks for card details.

## Third parties

Choosing a "Share on X" or "Share on Facebook" button opens that service. What you share there is covered by its own privacy policy. The Shop uses no analytics, advertising or tracking.

## Deleting your data

To delete all Shop data, open the Marketing tab and choose **Reset everything**. You can also clear the site's data in your browser, or uninstall the app.

## Children

The Shop isn't directed at children under 13.
